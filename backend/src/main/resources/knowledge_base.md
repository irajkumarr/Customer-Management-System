# Customer Support Knowledge Base

## 1. Billing & Subscriptions

### How do I update my payment method?

To update your payment method, log in to your dashboard, go to **Settings > Billing**, and click "Update Payment Info." You can add a new credit card or link a PayPal account. Changes take effect immediately for the next billing cycle.

### Where can I find my invoices?

Invoices are automatically emailed to your registered email address on the 1st of every month. You can also download past invoices from the **Billing History** section in your dashboard.

### What is your refund policy?

We offer a full refund within 30 days of your initial purchase if you are not satisfied with the service. After 30 days, refunds are handled on a case-by-case basis. To request a refund, please contact our support team with your Order ID.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel your subscription at any time by going to **Settings > Subscription** and clicking "Cancel Plan." Your access will remain active until the end of your current billing period.

## 2. Technical Support

### I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

Click the "Forgot Password?" link on the login page. Enter your registered email address, and we will send you a secure link to reset your password. The link expires after 24 hours.

### The website is loading very slowly. What should I do?

First, try clearing your browser cache and cookies. If the issue persists, check your internet connection. If you are still experiencing slow load times, please provide us with your browser type and version so our technical team can investigate.

### I am getting a "500 Internal Server Error."

This is usually a temporary issue on our end. Please wait 5 minutes and try refreshing the page. If the error continues, please take a screenshot of the error message and send it to our support team along with the time it occurred.

### Does your platform support Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)?

Yes! We strongly recommend enabling 2FA for added security. Go to **Settings > Security** and follow the instructions to link your authenticator app (like Google Authenticator or Authy).

## 3. Account Management

### How do I change my email address?

To change your email address, go to **Settings > Profile**. Enter your new email and click "Save." You will receive a verification email at the new address. You must click the link in that email to confirm the change.

### Can I have multiple users on one account?

Yes, our "Team" and "Enterprise" plans allow for multiple user seats. The account owner can invite new members via the **Team Management** section in the dashboard.

### How do I delete my account?

We are sorry to see you go. To permanently delete your account and all associated data, please submit a request through the **Privacy Center** or email us directly. Note that this action is irreversible.

## 4. General Policies

### What are your support hours?

Our AI support is available 24/7. Human agents are available Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM (EST). For urgent issues outside of these hours, please mark your ticket as "Urgent."

### Do you offer a free trial?

Yes, we offer a 14-day free trial for all new users. No credit card is required to start the trial. At the end of the 14 days, you will be prompted to choose a paid plan to continue using the service.

### How do I report a bug?

If you encounter a bug, please describe the steps to reproduce it, the expected behavior, and the actual behavior. Screenshots or screen recordings are highly appreciated. Send these details to our support email.
