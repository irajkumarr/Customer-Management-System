# Customer Support Knowledge Base

## 1. Billing & Subscriptions

### How do I update my payment method?

To update your payment method:
1. Log in to your customer dashboard.
2. Navigate to **Settings > Billing & Payments**.
3. Under the **Payment Methods** section, click **Update Payment Info** or **Add New Card**.
4. Enter your new credit/debit card details (card number, expiration date, and CVV) or link an authorized PayPal account.
5. Click **Save Changes**.

Your updated payment method will immediately be set as your primary billing method and will be charged on your next scheduled billing renewal date.

### Where can I find and download my invoices?

Invoices and payment receipts are generated automatically upon successful billing:
- **Email Delivery:** Invoices are sent directly to the account owner's registered billing email address within 24 hours of each payment.
- **Dashboard Download:** You can view, search, and download past invoices at any time. Go to **Settings > Billing**, scroll down to **Billing History / Invoices**, and click the **Download PDF** icon next to the relevant billing cycle date.
- **Missing Invoices:** If an invoice is missing or does not reflect your latest payment, contact our support team with your Account ID or transaction reference.

### What is your refund policy?

We strive to ensure complete customer satisfaction:
- **30-Day Money-Back Guarantee:** We offer a full, 100% refund on initial subscription purchases within 30 days of the original transaction date if you are not satisfied with the service.
- **Refunds After 30 Days:** Refund requests submitted after 30 days are evaluated on a case-by-case basis and require review by a human support specialist.
- **Processing Time:** Once approved, refunds typically take between 5 to 10 business days to appear on your bank or credit card statement, depending on your financial institution.
- **How to Request a Refund:** Submit a support ticket or email our billing team with your Order ID, account email, and the reason for your refund request.

### How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel your active subscription at any time without penalty or cancellation fees:
1. Log in to your account dashboard.
2. Navigate to **Settings > Subscription Plans**.
3. Click the **Cancel Plan** button at the bottom of the page.
4. Select a reason for cancellation and confirm your choice.

**Access Period:** After cancellation, your account will remain fully active with all plan features until the end of your current paid billing period. You will not be billed again. If you wish to reactivate before your billing cycle ends, simply click **Reactivate Subscription** in your billing settings.

### What subscription plans and pricing tiers do you offer?

We offer three flexible tiers tailored to different organizational needs:
- **Starter Plan:** Designed for individuals and small startups. Includes up to 3 user seats, core CRM contact management, basic email ticketing, and standard support.
- **Professional / Team Plan:** Ideal for growing customer service teams. Includes up to 15 user seats, AI-powered Cognitive Desk auto-replies, advanced analytics, custom tags, and priority email support.
- **Enterprise Plan:** Built for large scale operations. Unlimited user seats, dedicated account manager, custom API integrations, 99.9% uptime SLA, SSO/SAML login, and 24/7 phone and live agent support.

For custom seat counts, annual contract pricing, or enterprise procurement, contact our sales and enterprise team.

### How does upgrading or downgrading my plan work?

You can adjust your plan at any time from **Settings > Subscription Plans**:
- **Upgrades:** Upgrades take effect immediately so you can use new features right away. A prorated charge will be applied to cover the remaining days in your current billing cycle.
- **Downgrades:** Downgrading to a lower plan takes effect at the beginning of your next billing cycle. You will continue to have access to your current plan features until the end of the paid period.
- **User Seat Limits:** If downgrading to a plan with fewer allowed seats, ensure you deactivate extra team member seats before the change takes effect.

### What happens if a payment fails or my card is declined?

If your subscription renewal payment cannot be processed:
1. **Notification:** We will immediately send an email alert notifying you of the failed transaction.
2. **Automatic Retries:** Our billing system will automatically retry charging your card up to 3 times over a 7-day grace period.
3. **Grace Period:** During this 7-day grace period, your platform access and customer support operations remain uninterrupted.
4. **Resolution:** To prevent service interruption, update your payment details under **Settings > Billing** before the grace period expires.
5. **Suspension:** If payment is not completed after 7 days, your account will enter a suspended read-only state until valid payment is provided.

### What payment methods and currencies do you accept?

- **Accepted Methods:** We accept major international credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover), PayPal, and ACH/Wire transfers (available for annual Enterprise contracts).
- **Currencies:** All standard online transactions are processed in USD ($). For Enterprise customers requiring billing in EUR (€), GBP (£), or other local currencies, please request multi-currency invoicing through your account representative.

### Do you offer discounts for annual billing or non-profits?

- **Annual Billing Discount:** Switching from monthly to annual billing provides an automatic 20% discount on all subscription tiers.
- **Non-Profit & Educational Discount:** We offer an additional 15% discount for qualified non-profit organizations, educational institutions, and open-source projects. To apply, submit a ticket with proof of 501(c)(3) or equivalent charitable status.

### How do I update my billing address or add a VAT / Tax ID?

To add your company tax details to your invoices:
1. Go to **Settings > Billing > Billing Information**.
2. Enter your legal company name, registered business address, and Tax ID / VAT Number.
3. Click **Save Billing Details**.
All future invoices will automatically include these tax details for accounting and compliance purposes.

## 2. Technical Support

### I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

If you cannot access your account:
1. Go to the login page and click the **Forgot Password?** link.
2. Enter your registered email address and submit the form.
3. Check your email inbox for a secure password reset link (ensure you check Spam/Junk if not found within 2 minutes).
4. Click the link and enter a new password (must be at least 8 characters and include a mixture of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols).
5. The password reset link expires after 24 hours for security reasons. If the link expires, simply request a new one.

### How do I enable and manage Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)?

Enhance your account security with Two-Factor Authentication:
1. Log in and navigate to **Settings > Security**.
2. Locate **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)** and click **Enable 2FA**.
3. Scan the displayed QR code using any standard authenticator app (such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Authy, or 1Password).
4. Enter the 6-digit verification code generated by the app and click **Verify & Enable**.
5. **Backup Codes:** Save the generated emergency backup recovery codes in a secure location. These can be used to log in if you lose access to your primary mobile device.

### What should I do if I lost access to my 2FA authenticator app?

If you lost your phone or cannot access your authenticator app:
1. **Use Backup Codes:** On the 2FA verification screen, click **Use Backup Recovery Code** and enter one of your saved single-use recovery codes.
2. **Contact Support for Identity Verification:** If you do not have your backup recovery codes, submit a ticket or email support. For security reasons, resetting 2FA requires human escalation and identity verification (verifying billing history or registered phone number) to protect your account from unauthorized takeover.

### The website is loading slowly or freezing. What should I do?

If you experience slow loading, lag, or visual glitches:
1. **Clear Browser Cache & Cookies:** Clear your browser's stored cache and cookies for the application domain, then restart your browser.
2. **Hard Refresh:** Perform a hard refresh using `Ctrl + F5` (Windows/Linux) or `Cmd + Shift + R` (macOS).
3. **Test in Incognito Mode:** Open an Incognito/Private window to verify if third-party browser extensions or ad blockers are causing conflicts.
4. **Network & Firewalls:** Verify that corporate VPNs or strict network proxies are not throttling WebSocket or REST API requests.
5. **System Diagnostics:** If the issue persists, submit a support ticket specifying your browser version, operating system, and the specific dashboard page experiencing slowdowns.

### I am getting a "500 Internal Server Error" or service error.

A 500 Internal Server Error indicates an unexpected temporary failure on the server side:
1. **Wait and Refresh:** Most 500 errors are transient and resolve automatically within 2 to 5 minutes as backend services recover or auto-scale.
2. **Check System Status:** Check our status page or announcements to verify if there is scheduled maintenance in progress.
3. **Report the Incident:** If the error persists after 10 minutes, take a screenshot of the error screen and note the exact time, the action you were attempting, and the URL. Submit a ticket so our engineering team can inspect server error logs and resolve the issue.

### What browsers and operating systems are supported?

Our platform is engineered for modern desktop and mobile browsers:
- **Recommended Browsers:** Google Chrome (v100+), Mozilla Firefox (v100+), Microsoft Edge (v100+), and Apple Safari (v15+).
- **Mobile Browsers:** Mobile Safari on iOS and Chrome for Android.
- **Unsupported:** Internet Explorer (IE 11 and older) is not supported. For optimal performance, always maintain your browser on the latest stable release with JavaScript enabled.

### Why are automated support or notification emails not arriving?

If you or your customers are not receiving email notifications:
1. **Spam & Junk Folders:** Verify if emails were filtered into Spam, Promotions, or Junk folders.
2. **Sender Whitelisting:** Add our system domain (`@domain.com`) and Mailgun dispatch addresses to your email client's safe sender list.
3. **Corporate Email Filters:** Certain corporate email exchange servers (e.g., Office 365, Mimecast, Barracuda) enforce strict anti-phishing rules. Contact your IT administrator to ensure SPF and DKIM records for our domain are approved.
4. **Bounced or Suppressed Addresses:** If an email address previously reported a hard bounce or spam complaint, it may be placed on an automated suppression list. Contact our support team to clear the suppression flag.

### Does the platform offer an API, and what are the rate limits?

Yes, our platform provides a RESTful API for managing customers, tickets, and messages:
- **Authentication:** All API calls require an API Bearer Token generated under **Settings > Developer & API Keys**.
- **Rate Limits:** Standard accounts have a rate limit of 60 requests per minute per API key. Professional accounts receive 300 requests per minute, and Enterprise accounts receive custom high-throughput limits.
- **Exceeding Limits:** When rate limits are exceeded, the API responds with HTTP status `429 Too Many Requests` along with a `Retry-After` response header.
- **Documentation:** Full interactive Swagger / OpenAPI documentation is accessible from the developer portal in your dashboard.

### How do webhooks and outbound notifications work?

- **Inbound Webhooks:** Inbound customer emails are received and parsed in real time via Mailgun webhooks. Each incoming message is verified via HMAC-SHA256 signature verification before ticket creation.
- **Outbound Webhooks:** You can configure custom webhooks under **Settings > Webhooks** to notify external services (such as Slack, Discord, or internal ERPs) when tickets are created, updated, or resolved.
- **Retry Mechanism:** If your webhook endpoint returns a non-2xx status code, our system retries delivery with exponential backoff up to 5 times.

### How do I report a software bug or unexpected behavior?

To help our technical team diagnose and fix bugs promptly, please provide:
1. **Summary:** A concise description of the bug.
2. **Steps to Reproduce:** Numbered step-by-step instructions starting from login.
3. **Expected vs. Actual Outcome:** What you anticipated happening compared to what occurred.
4. **Environment:** Operating system, browser name, and browser version.
5. **Attachments:** Screenshots, screen recordings, or browser console error logs (`F12 > Console`).
Submit this information via an in-app support ticket or email our technical support desk.

## 3. Account Management

### How do I change my registered email address?

To update the primary email address on your account:
1. Log in and go to **Settings > Profile**.
2. In the **Email Address** field, enter your new email.
3. Click **Save Profile**.
4. A security verification link will be sent to the **new email address**.
5. You must click the confirmation link in that email within 24 hours to finalize the change. For security reasons, your original email address will also receive a notification alerting you of the pending email update.

### How do I invite team members and manage user seats?

Account owners and administrators can invite additional staff:
1. Navigate to **Settings > Team Management**.
2. Click **Invite Team Member**.
3. Enter the member's email address, full name, and assign their role (e.g., Administrator, Support Agent, or Viewer).
4. Click **Send Invitation**.
The recipient will receive an invitation email containing a secure link to join your organization and create their login credentials. If you exceed your plan's user seat limit, you will be prompted to add more seats before sending the invite.

### What roles and permissions are available for team members?

We provide role-based access control (RBAC) to ensure security and compliance:
- **Account Owner:** Full authority across the entire platform, including billing, plan cancellations, security settings, and inviting/removing admins.
- **Administrator:** Can manage customers, tickets, team members, integrations, webhooks, and view analytics. Cannot delete the account owner or cancel the company subscription.
- **Support Agent:** Can view, assign, reply to, and resolve customer tickets, view customer profiles, and add internal notes.
- **Viewer / Read-Only:** Can browse tickets and analytics dashboards for audit or reporting purposes, but cannot send customer replies, modify tickets, or change system settings.

### How do I transfer account ownership to another person?

To transfer the Account Owner role to another team member:
1. Ensure the new owner is already an active member of your organization under **Settings > Team Management**.
2. Go to **Settings > Account Details > Ownership Transfer**.
3. Select the team member and click **Request Ownership Transfer**.
4. Both the current owner and the new owner must confirm the transfer via email verification links.
5. If the current owner has left the company and cannot access their account, submit a ticket with legal documentation (company authorization letter) so our support team can review and process the transfer manually.

### How do I export my company data, customer records, and tickets?

You can export your organization's data at any time:
1. Navigate to **Settings > Data & Privacy > Export Data**.
2. Select the datasets you wish to export:
   - Customer Contact Records (CSV)
   - Support Ticket History & Conversations (CSV or JSON)
   - Performance Metrics & Analytics Reports (CSV)
3. Click **Generate Export**.
Large exports will be compiled in the background, and a secure download link will be emailed to the account administrator within 15 to 30 minutes. Download links remain valid for 7 days.

### How do I permanently delete my account and data (GDPR Right to Erasure)?

We respect your privacy and comply with GDPR, CCPA, and global data privacy standards:
1. **Submission:** To request permanent account deletion and complete erasure of personal data, the Account Owner must go to **Settings > Privacy Center** and click **Request Permanent Deletion**, or email our privacy team directly.
2. **Human Verification:** Because account deletion is irreversible and deletes all customer records, ticket history, vector embeddings, and billing records, an escalation ticket is generated for human verification to prevent accidental data loss.
3. **Data Retention & Purge:** Once verified, your account is immediately deactivated and all operational data is permanently wiped from our primary and backup databases within 30 days in accordance with data protection regulations.

### Why was I automatically logged out of my session?

For security and protection against unauthorized session hijacking:
- **Inactivity Timeout:** User sessions automatically expire after 8 hours of inactivity.
- **Single Admin Session:** If your account is configured for single-session administrative access, logging in from a new browser or device will automatically terminate previous active sessions.
- **Password Updates:** Changing your password or updating 2FA immediately invalidates all existing active sessions across all devices, requiring you to log in again.

### How do I update my personal profile and notification preferences?

1. Go to **Settings > Profile**.
2. Update your display name, contact phone number, avatar, and preferred timezone.
3. Under **Notification Settings**, configure your alerts for new customer tickets, escalation notices, customer email replies, and daily digest summaries.
4. Click **Save Preferences**.

## 4. General Policies & SLA

### What are your support hours and channels?

We offer multi-tiered support to keep your operations running smoothly:
- **AI Helpdesk Support (Cognitive Desk):** Available 24/7/365. Automatically answers queries, references documentation, and assists customers in real time.
- **Human Support Specialists:** Available Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST / UTC-5), excluding major US public holidays.
- **Emergency On-Call Support:** Critical system outages and Enterprise P1 urgent issues are monitored by on-call engineers 24/7.
- **Support Channels:** In-app ticket submission, customer portal, and email support at `support@domain.com`.

### How do support ticket priorities and response time SLAs work?

Tickets are classified by urgency and impact, with guaranteed initial response targets during business hours:
- **Urgent (P1):** Complete system outage, data loss risk, or critical production failure affecting all users. Response target: **Under 1 hour** (24/7 coverage).
- **High (P2):** Core functionality degraded or billing error preventing operations with no workaround. Response target: **Under 4 business hours**.
- **Medium (P3):** Non-critical feature issue, minor bug, or account inquiry with an available workaround. Response target: **Under 8 business hours**.
- **Low (P4):** General inquiries, documentation questions, or feature feedback. Response target: **Under 24 business hours**.

### Do you offer a free trial, and what are the terms?

Yes! We provide a comprehensive **14-day free trial** for all new organizations:
- **No Credit Card Required:** You do not need to provide payment information to start your trial.
- **Full Access:** Explore core CRM customer management, email ingestion, and our AI Cognitive Desk ticketing pipeline.
- **Trial Expiration:** At the end of the 14 days, you can choose a paid subscription plan to maintain uninterrupted access. If you decide not to subscribe, your account will pause and your data will be safely archived for 30 days before deletion.

### How does the AI Helpdesk (Cognitive Desk) handle support requests?

Our AI Helpdesk uses Google Gemini paired with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) over our official knowledge base:
1. When an email or ticket is received, the AI analyzes the subject and customer message.
2. It queries our pgvector semantic knowledge base for the most relevant documentation chunks.
3. It categorizes the ticket (`BILLING`, `TECHNICAL`, `ACCOUNT`, or `GENERAL`).
4. If the knowledge base provides high-confidence evidence, the AI formulates an empathetic, accurate reply, sets the ticket status to `AI_REPLIED`, and emails the customer.
5. If the request requires human intervention, missing evidence, or custom decisions, the AI immediately flags the ticket for human escalation.

### When will my ticket be escalated to a human agent?

Our AI agent operates with strict safety guardrails and will automatically escalate your ticket to a human support specialist (`requiresHuman: true`) in the following scenarios:
- **Missing or Ambiguous Information:** Any inquiry where the answer is not documented with high confidence in the approved knowledge base.
- **Account Deletion Requests:** Permanent deletion of company accounts or GDPR data erasure requests.
- **Custom Enterprise Inquiries:** Custom contractual pricing, annual SLAs, or high-volume procurement requests.
- **Refund Requests Beyond 30 Days:** Non-standard refund requests exceeding the initial 30-day money-back guarantee.
- **Security & Account Recovery:** Requests to bypass or reset 2FA without backup codes, or disputed account ownership transfers.
- **Customer Frustration or Explicit Human Request:** When a customer specifically requests a human agent or reports repeated unresolved technical issues.

When a ticket is escalated, its status changes to `ESCALATED_TO_HUMAN`, an alert is posted to the support dashboard, and the customer receives a confirmation email indicating a team member is reviewing the case.

### What are your security, encryption, and data privacy standards?

We prioritize enterprise-grade security and data privacy across all layers:
- **Encryption in Transit:** All traffic is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
- **Encryption at Rest:** Sensitive customer databases, vector stores, and backup archives are encrypted using AES-256 encryption.
- **Regulatory Compliance:** Fully compliant with General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).
- **Data Isolation:** Customer databases and support tickets are isolated per organization with strict tenant authorization checks.
- **AI Privacy Guarantee:** Customer queries and support emails sent to our AI helpdesk are processed in secure, zero-data-retention enterprise inference environments and are never used to train public machine learning models.

### How do I submit product feature requests or feedback?

We welcome ideas and suggestions from our users:
1. Submit your feedback through our **Feedback & Feature Portal** under **Help > Suggest a Feature**, or email `feedback@domain.com`.
2. Please explain your use case, the problem you are solving, and how this feature would improve your daily workflow.
3. Our product team reviews all feature submissions monthly. Highly requested items are prioritized on our public product roadmap.
